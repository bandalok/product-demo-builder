# Auto-Triage for the Support Inbox

## Problem
Support agents at growing B2B companies drown in an unsorted shared inbox.
Urgent customer issues wait hours behind password resets, and nobody knows
which ticket will breach SLA next.

## Target users
Support leads and agents at B2B SaaS companies with 50 to 500 seats.

## Key features
- AI urgency scoring on every incoming ticket
- One-click suggested replies drafted from past resolutions
- SLA risk radar that surfaces tickets about to breach
- Auto-routing by topic to the right queue

## Success metrics
- First-response time down 40% within 30 days
- SLA breaches down 60% within 60 days
- Agent touches per ticket from 4 to 2
